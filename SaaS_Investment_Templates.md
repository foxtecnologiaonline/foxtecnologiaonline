## 7. Follow-up Sequence for Investor Conversations

**After initial meeting:**

Day 1: Send thank you email + relevant materials
- "Thanks for taking the time! As promised, here's our deck and traction data."

Day 3: Check-in email
- "How are you thinking about this? Any questions I can answer?"

Day 7: Additional context
- "Wanted to share our latest metrics: [updated numbers]"

Day 14: Next step proposal
- "Would next week work to introduce you to [customer/team member]?"

Day 21: Gentle close
- "Where do we stand? What else can I do to help you decide?"

Day 30: Move on or pivot
- Either: "When do you think you'll decide?" (if seriously interested)
- Or: "Thanks anyway, staying in touch!" (if not interested)

## 10. Red Flags to Avoid

### Financial Red Flags:
- ❌ Revenue trending down
- ❌ Churn rising above 5%
- ❌ CAC payback > 24 months
- ❌ Negative unit economics
- ❌ Runway < 6 months
- ❌ Inaccurate financial forecasting

### Product Red Flags:
- ❌ NPS < 30
- ❌ No clear differentiation vs. competitors
- ❌ Slow to iterate on feedback
- ❌ Security/compliance issues
- ❌ Technical debt preventing scaling

### Team Red Flags:
- ❌ Founder churn or conflict
- ❌ No prior startup experience
- ❌ Weak domain expertise
- ❌ Poor execution track record
- ❌ Key person dependency

### Presentation Red Flags:
- ❌ Overly optimistic projections (100%+ growth)
- ❌ Vague answers to due diligence questions
- ❌ Inconsistent metrics across presentations
- ❌ Dishonest about competition
- ❌ Unwilling to share customer references
